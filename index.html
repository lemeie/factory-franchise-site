A plan for local electronics manufacturing.

Factory Franchise plans locally owned electronics factories across Europe, targeting first boards six months after contract.

Factory Franchise plans electronics factories across Europe, owned by the people who run them with a six-month setup target.

One line,
every station.

The planned package covers a complete SMT line: printer, inspection, placement, reflow and optical inspection, with import, CE conformity checks and installation.

One standard.
Every site.

The concept uses one documented process, with preparation for the standards customers require, from ISO 9001 to IATF 16949. Each site would need its own certification.

  • Line status. The preview shows each station of the line and whether it is running.

  • Training. The preview shows operator training progress.
  • Component quotes. The preview shows supplier quotes and an order button.
  • Maintenance. The preview shows a due task and a booking button.
